# BucRater app shell (Libros / Métricas / Tags)

Mode: Operate. Single user, personal reading diary, laptop or narrow window, light theme by default with dark available.
Job: log, find and revisit read books; look back at the year. Structure, flows and copy are product truth; the visual world is replaced.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The library is a run of clothbound books, not a dashboard of cards. Covers stand face-out on shelves; each book wears a cloth board coloured by its first tag, its title stamped in foil. Refuses the category default: neutral white grid of shadowed cover cards with a sidebar of filters.

OWN-WORLD: Book cloth fields (bottle green, oxblood, ink blue, ochre) as solid planes; linen ground with a fine weave; gold foil for stamped type, stars and the active state; blind-debossed (inset) controls; striped headbands as the only ornament; endpaper pattern behind the detail view. Display: Gloock (stamped lettering). UI: Schibsted Grotesk. Authored stroke icons, no glyphs.

STORY: The owner sees their shelf, finds a book in seconds, opens it like lifting it off the shelf, edits, and at year end reads their year as a stamped colophon.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width bottle-green cloth header band with the gold-foil BucRater wordmark left, debossed nav tabs centre (gold foil underline slides), actions right. Below, a stamped filter strip, then books face-out on shelves, each row resting on a ledge; "Añadir libro" is the one oxblood cloth button. A Portadas/Lomos switch turns the shelf into spines (thickness = pages).

FORM: Encuadernación en tela, position 1 of my ordered list (Impeccable's pick), seed key 8e031e32. Raises kept: segmented detail ledger with tabular figures; layout-preserving re-sort; per-digit count-up in the year summary; status encoded by fill as well as colour; chart bars keep identity and morph between years instead of replaying.

SIGNATURE MOTION: the cover lifts from the shelf into the detail view (shared layout), endpaper fades in behind; spines slide into place when switching views; foil underline glides between tabs.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
